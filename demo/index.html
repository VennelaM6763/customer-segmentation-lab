Method and limitations

RFM is computed from positive sales after removing exact duplicates, cancellations, negative quantities, and zero-price lines. Customers without IDs cannot be segmented. Frequency counts invoices, not line items. Monetary value excludes refunds, and the recency snapshot is one day after the final transaction date.

K-means is fitted on all eligible historical customers. Candidate k values run from 2 to 6 with 20 initializations each. The selected k has the highest silhouette on a fixed sample of up to 2,000 customers. A second random seed checks sensitivity to initialization. Log transformation reduces skew without deleting high-value customers.

This is descriptive segmentation with no ground-truth labels. Segment membership is not a probability or a causal finding. Marketing actions would require experiments; future customer behavior and cluster stability over time have not been validated.
